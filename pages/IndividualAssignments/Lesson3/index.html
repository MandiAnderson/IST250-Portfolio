Collectable Mugs for Sale!

Etched Stoneware Mugs from Zoos and Aquariums
from Around the United States

All mugs are priced at $20, or best offer.

How It All Started

My collection started because of my love for the artstyle the animals were depicted in on the mugs.
Each time I visited my local zoo, the Philadelphia Zoo, I purchased a mug if they carried a new design.

When I travelled to other zoos and aquariums, I noticed they sold mugs in this style in their gift shops too!
Every time I visited a zoo, I made a visit to their gift shop in hopes of adding another mug to my collection.

Every mug has been purchased from an AZA (Association of Zoos and Aquariums) accredited zoo or aquarium.


Click on the AZA logo or more information about this accredidation.

Spending money in gift shops is also a great way of supporting these facilities and their conservation efforts.

Mugs are listed in order of purchase.
