How It All Started

Media Music is a family-owned record store located in Anytown, USA. We have been in business for over 20 years and pride ourselves on our extensive collection of vinyl records. Our store is a haven for music lovers of all ages and genres, and we are passionate about sharing our love of music with our customers. This store location was founded in 2013 because our family wanted to shared our love of music with the community and wanted to create a space where people could come together to discover new artists and connect with other music enthusiasts. Over the years, we have built a loyal customer base and have become a staple in the local music scene.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our customers with a unique and personalized shopping experience. We believe that music is more than just a product, it's a way of life. We strive to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for all music lovers. We are committed to providing our customers with high-quality vinyl records, as well as exceptional customer service. We want our customers to feel like they are part of a community when they shop with us, and we are always looking for ways to connect with our customers and support local artists.

Our knowledgeable staff is always happy to help customers find the perfect album or recommend new music based on their tastes. If you ever have any questions, don't be afraid to ask. We're here to help you not only find what you came into the store looking for, but also discover some new favorites along the way.

Thank you for supporting our small business!
